Not sure? Start with Plus. Moving to Ultra later is just a matter of swapping one folder, and your games, saves and settings stay put. Moving back works the same way.

  1. Flash BaseOS to an SD Card

    Download the latest release of BaseOS and write the .img to an SD card with something like RUFUS or Raspberry Pi Imager.

    Insert this card into the SD card slot labeled SD1.

  2. Expand the storage

    After inserting the SD card, power on your device. You should see a BaseOS boot logo and a message saying that storage is being expanded.

    Once the storage is expanded, a message will say that no frontend was found. Power off your device.

  3. Download the latest release

    Fetch slot-plus.zip or slot-plusultra.zip from the latest release.

  4. Copy it onto your SD Card

    Eject the SD card from SD1 and connect it back to your computer. Unzip the release and copy the contents to the root of the SD card.

  5. Add your games

    Drop your legally obtained ROM files into their system's folder in Games/. The Systems tab lists every folder and file type. You may optionally place labels into the matching folder in Labels/.

    ROMs must be unzipped!

    slotPlus does not read .zip or .7z files.

    Atari Lynx needs its boot ROM

    Put your own copy of lynxboot.img in BIOS/. It is not included. No other system needs a BIOS.

  6. Re-insert the card into SD1 and boot!

    Each system's folders under Games, Labels, Saves, States and Bezels are created on first boot, if they aren't there already.

Already playing on slot? slotPlus drops in over it. Everything you have stays: games, saves, save states, labels, wallpapers, favorites and settings. You can go back to slot the same way.

  1. Download Plus or Ultra

    Pick a version on the Which Version? tab and unzip it.

  2. Replace the System/ folder

    Connect the card that holds slot to your computer. That is the card in SD1 if you use a single card, or the card in SD2 if you use two.

    Delete the System/ folder on the card and copy the new System/ folder from the zip in its place. Only the System/ folder changes. Everything else on the card is yours and is left alone.

  3. Playing Lynx games? Add the boot ROM

    Put your own copy of lynxboot.img in BIOS/. No other system needs one.

  4. Put the card back and boot

    slotPlus creates the new systems' folders under Games, Labels, Saves, States and Bezels. Fill Games/ and the new shelves appear.

Switching between Plus and Ultra works the same way: replace System/ with the other version's. Going from Ultra back to Plus leaves the Game Gear, NeoGeo Pocket, TurboGrafx-16 and DS games and saves on the card, just without their shelves.

  1. Download the latest release

    Fetch the same version you're running, Plus or Ultra, from the latest release and unzip it.

  2. Replace the System/ folder

    Connect the card that holds slotPlus to your computer, delete its System/ folder and copy the new one in its place. Your games, saves, save states, labels, bezels and wallpapers all live outside System/ and are left alone.

  3. Put the card back and boot!

What slotPlus adds

What slotPlus adds to slot. For everything else, slot's own guide has you covered.

  1. PlusUltra

    More Shelves

    L1 and R1 switch shelves, just like slot.
    Plus adds WonderSwan and Atari Lynx.
    Ultra adds Game Gear, NeoGeo Pocket, TurboGrafx-16 and Nintendo DS.

  2. PlusUltra

    A Cart for Every System

    Each system gets its own cart, drawn in slot's look.
    WonderSwan games come in black, WonderSwan Color games in frosted clear.
    Give any game its own shell in Config/cart_shell.txt.

  3. PlusUltra

    A Cart Slot for Every System

    Set Settings > General > Cart Slot to Bespoke and the slot on each shelf becomes the back of that system's handheld.
    Plus has a pearl WonderSwan and a Lynx II. Ultra adds a Game Gear, a carbon NeoGeo Pocket Color, a TurboExpress and a silver DS Lite.
    The Game Boy shelves keep slot's own.

  4. Ultra

    HuCards Go In Contacts First

    A HuCard turns end over end before it slides into the slot.
    It turns back upright when you eject it.

  5. Ultra

    Two Screens and a Stylus

    DS games play on DSperate, at full speed.
    L2 swaps the main screen, and SELECT + L2 steps through Both Screens, One Screen, Full Screen, Side by Side and Picture in Picture.
    Hold R2 and the D-pad moves a stylus over the bottom screen, and A touches.

  6. PlusUltra

    Games Made to Be Held Sideways

    Vertical WonderSwan games and sideways Lynx games are drawn turned, for playing with the handheld on its side.
    START on a Lynx cart turns it over to choose how you'll hold it.
    SELECT stands the picture upright instead.

  7. PlusUltra

    Stretch Every System

    L1 stretches Game Gear, NeoGeo Pocket and TurboGrafx-16 games.
    R1 puts them back.
    WonderSwan and Lynx games stretch with a line in Config/video_mode.txt.

  8. PlusUltra

    Shaders and Bezels for Each System

    Each system has its own page under Screen, with its own Shader and Bezel.
    SELECT + B / A steps through that system's shaders.
    Ultra bundles bezels for the Game Gear, NeoGeo Pocket, TurboGrafx-16 and Nintendo DS.

  9. Every version

    Pixel Transparency

    A new Transparent shader for Game Boy and Game Boy Color.
    Light pixels let a warm, grainy backing show through, and dark pixels cast soft shadows onto it.

  10. Every version

    HDMI Support

    Plug a TV into the mini-HDMI port and the shelf and games move to it, sound and all.
    Pull the cable mid-game and slotPlus saves, moves back to the handheld and carries on.

Systems

What every added system has in common. The Game Boy systems work exactly as they do in slot.

Shelf
Each system gets its own shelf. Its folders under Games, Labels, Saves, States and Bezels are created on first boot.
Label art
Optional PNGs in Labels/<folder>/<game name>.png, named after the game's file without its extension. Art of another size is scaled to cover the label and cropped, except on HuCards, which keep the art's own shape.
Controls
The In-Game controls page in Settings covers every system, so any of the buttons below can be moved. The tables show where they start.
Screen menu
Each system has its own page under Screen, with its own Shader and Bezel, beside General for Brightness and Blue Light. SELECT + B / A in a game steps through that system's shaders.
Your own bezels
PNGs in Bezels/<folder>/ the size of the screen, with the screen area transparent: 720x480, or 640x480 on a 4:3 handheld such as the RG 35XXSP, where it shows pixel for pixel and the SP frame is offered too. A bezel shows at actual size, not when the picture is stretched.
Stretch
Game Gear, NeoGeo Pocket and TurboGrafx-16: L1 stretches, R1 gives back actual size. Lynx and WonderSwan use L1 and R1 as game buttons, so a game is set to stretch in Config/video_mode.txt, as Game Name (USA) = stretch. Each game remembers its choice. The Nintendo DS has screen layouts instead.
Cart shells
Each system's carts have a default shell. Config/cart_shell.txt gives a single game its own.
States and resume
Save states and the resume state work as they do for every other system.

Games that turn

Some Lynx games were made to be held on their side, one way or the other. These start with the picture drawn the way the game is made, at full size: turn the handheld the way it was meant to be held and the game is upright, with the D-pad following it. Beetle Lynx knows many of these games, and slotPlus also recognises Klax, Gauntlet III, Lexis and NFL Football by name.

Choosing a rotation

On the shelf, press START on a Lynx cart to turn it over. Its back has a socket for each way you can hold the handheld: Upright, 90CCW, 90CW and 180. Move the chip with the D-pad and press A to keep it, or B to leave it as it was. The game then starts upright for the way you chose, with the D-pad turned to match, whichever way it was made to be held. In a game, SELECT switches to the picture upright and back.

For a sideways game slotPlus doesn't recognise, add a line to Config/lynx_rotation.txt so it knows which way the game is made:

Game Name (USA) = 90

The values are auto, 0, 90, 270, 180 and manual. If a game comes up upside down, swap 90 for 270. With manual, each SELECT turns the picture another quarter, so you can find the right value.

Studio+

Labels for every system on your card, all eight of them, previewed on the exact cart slotPlus draws. Pick your SD card and it finds your games, gives each a logo on a colour taken from its box art, and lets you change the art and the shell before writing the labels straight back.

Every game looks in slotPlus's own art set first, which fills in each day from ScreenScraper with labels and logos for the WonderSwan, Atari Lynx, Game Gear, NeoGeo Pocket and TurboGrafx-16. After that the Game Boys try slot's art set, every system tries libretro's logos, and a game with no art anywhere gets its title screen. Any cart can take your own logo or label.
